# Objective **
The core objective of the project is to ensure people with an automated system of helping 
seeking in profession and other field of emergency with efficiency and transparency to 
reduce the hurdle with an optimized process. This will helps people to find out the 
professional who need help. And for the professional to find out the job or work in the field 
of his/her professional field and can provide the needy. Overall, the system’s targeted aims 
is to automate the world of cooperating each other by reducing time consumption, struggle, 
manual error and ensure the fairness an transparency in the cooperation  process. 
# Expected Outcome **
The main goal of the project is to build an automated system for exam room allocation and 
seat allocation. The system contains two types of users: teacher & administrator. The 
system will enable the teachers to upload the information about the course to be examined, 
such as course code, teacher name & teacher initial level and term of the course, course 
section and total students as well as total registered students for the exam. And for the 
admin they can create, update, delete rooms, courses, add routine, allocate rooms and 
allocate seats according to the rooms. Finally, the system will provide a downloadable 
report of allocated rooms and seats. As a result, it will remove the old traditional way of 
manually allocating rooms and seats.
